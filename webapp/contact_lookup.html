Before you start

Please read and accept the user agreement.

Lookups run under your own (or your team's) account with a contact-data provider. hooconnex passes the request on and shows the answer; it does not store the email addresses, phone numbers or addresses that come back. It records who looked up whom, with which provider, and how many items came back. Personal details (home address, personal email, mobile) are never available for judges, law enforcement, prosecutors or election officials, or for anyone who has switched lookups about themselves off.
