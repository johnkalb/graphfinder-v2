sixdegrees.net user agreement

Version beta-1 · applies to the private links and question features in the beta
Draft, pending legal review. This text will be reviewed by a lawyer before the beta opens beyond its first testers. Material changes will be shown to you for acceptance again.

sixdegrees.net (“the service”, “we”) maps connections between people and organizations using public records and automated matching. By selecting “I agree” you accept this agreement. If you don't agree, don't use the private links or question features.

1. Beta, free, no commitment

This is a beta. It is free while we test it and is not a subscription. We collect no payment details. Features, limits (currently 10 questions a day) and availability may change or end at any time, without notice. Prices may be shown next to questions to indicate what they would cost; you are not charged.

2. What the data is — and isn't

  • Connections come from public records (for example campaign-finance, nonprofit, corporate, lobbying, court and patent filings, news and Wikidata) and from automated name matching. They can be wrong. Different people can share a name; records can be outdated or mis-filed.
  • A link between two people means they appear together in a record. It is not evidence that they know each other, did anything wrong, or have any particular relationship.
  • Answers are provided “as is”, without warranty of accuracy, completeness or fitness for any purpose. Check anything important against the original records.

3. Acceptable use

You agree not to use the service:

  • to harass, stalk, threaten, intimidate or embarrass anyone, or to locate a person for that purpose;
  • to make or support decisions about a person's employment, housing, credit, insurance, education or eligibility for any benefit — the service is not a consumer reporting agency and its data must not be used as a background check;
  • to compile profiles of private individuals, or to extract, scrape or copy data in bulk;
  • to add false links, impersonate anyone, or interfere with the service or other users;
  • for anything unlawful.

Questions about private (non-public-figure) people are limited to how people are connected; we log such questions and may limit or suspend accounts that misuse the service.

4. Your links

  • Private links (the default) are visible only to you and used only in your own searches.
  • Public links are allowed only to verified public figures, up to 50, and are visible to everyone, labelled “self-reported”. Other users can report a public link they believe is false.
  • Only add links to people you actually know. You are responsible for the links you add.
  • You can change a link between private and public, delete it, or download all your links at any time. Deleting takes effect immediately.

5. Privacy

  • We store your links and questions on our servers so the service can use them. Private links are hidden from other users. Our operators do not browse private data; any administrative access is logged, and private data is excluded from administrative screens and exports.
  • We keep a log of the questions you ask (to run the daily limit and prevent misuse) and the suggestions you send (to decide what to build).
  • If you delete your account, your links (private and public) and your acceptance of this agreement are deleted; question logs are kept only as anonymized counts.
  • We don't sell your data.

We may change this agreement; for material changes we will ask you to accept the new version before you continue. We may suspend or end your access at any time, especially for misuse. You may stop using the service and delete your account at any time.
