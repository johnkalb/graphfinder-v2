What you can do

🔎

Look up anyone in the record

Search a CEO, judge, senator, donor or nonprofit board member and see their documented ties: boards, schools, investments, campaigns and court cases.

🧭

Find the path between two people

Enter two names and hooconnex finds the shortest chain of real connections between them. Every link shows the record it came from.

📇

"How do I reach…?"

Add the people you know. If any of them appear in the public record, hooconnex shows which one is closest to the person you want to meet, and who the next introduction would be. Your contacts stay private to you.

Where the connections come from

  • Corporate boards and officers
  • SEC filings
  • Political donations
  • Lobbying disclosures
  • Nonprofit filings
  • Court records
  • Schools and alumni
  • News coverage
  • Wikidata

Refreshed every day. A link means two people appear together in a record, not that they know each other well.
